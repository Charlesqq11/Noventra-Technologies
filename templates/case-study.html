Industry Logistics & Operations

01 — OVERVIEW

Bringing inventory and operational workflows together.

The platform is designed around a common operational challenge: businesses need a clear way to monitor inventory, identify materials that require replenishment and manage requests between teams.

Noventra approached the concept as a centralized web application where operational information can be organized, monitored and acted upon from one place.

02

BUSINESS CHALLENGE

Turning operational complexity into a clearer workflow.

Inventory-related processes can involve multiple teams, requests and approval steps. Without a centralized workflow, important information can become difficult to track.

The solution concept focuses on giving teams visibility into inventory levels and helping identify materials that require attention.

03

OUR SOLUTION

A centralized digital operations platform.

We structured the platform around inventory visibility, material requests and automated workflow preparation. The interface is designed to make important information easy to find and act upon.

Inventory Monitoring

Track available materials and identify inventory that requires replenishment.

Material Requests

Organize requests for materials through a centralized workflow.

Reorder Detection

Identify spare parts that fall below defined reorder levels.

Operational Dashboard

Present important operational information in a clear dashboard interface.

PROJECT OUTCOME

A clearer foundation for digital operations.

The concept demonstrates how a centralized web application can organize inventory information, material requests and operational workflows.

The architecture can also be extended with additional features such as authentication, reporting, notifications, integrations and advanced analytics.
